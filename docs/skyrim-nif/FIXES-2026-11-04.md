# Business Woman — fixes after the first in-game look

Date: 2026-11-04. Follows `AUDIT-2026-11-04.md` (which is a measurement pass, not
a fix) and answers the four in-game screenshots: floating gloves, a bare gap
between cuff and glove, torn fabric on the upper arm / shoulder, and white
"wings" flaring over the shoulders.

Everything below is measured on the shipped model
(`build/skyrim-nif/business_woman.model`, rebuilt from `4.zip` + the 2B
template) and re-rendered offline in the pose that shows the defects
(`tools/pd2-las/pose_preview.py`, arms adducted). Two new checks were written for
this round: `tools/pd2-las/pose_zoom.py` (shoulder close-up) and
`tools/pd2-las/glove_check.py` (sleeve/glove junction with a glove proxy placed
on the PD2 hand bone).

## Root cause 1 — the glove was never reachable (fixed)

The 3BA source is authored to be worn *with hands*: every layer on the arm stops
short of the wrist so the wearer's own hand mesh covers the junction. PD2 does
not work that way — the heister's glove is spawned **at** the hand joint, so the
outfit has to reach it. Nothing in the pipeline did: `extend_blazer_sleeves`
stretched the blazer by 3 cm, which is 3 cm of the 6.3 cm that were missing, and
`tuck_shirt_cuffs` pulled the one layer that *did* reach further up the arm.

`build_models.extend_sleeves_to_wrist` redistributes the last 13 cm of each arm
layer along the forearm axis (rank-1 stretch: the cross-section, and therefore
the cuff radius, is untouched) until the layer reaches its target:

| layer | target (distance past the PD2 hand joint) | why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| blazer (`jacket`) | −2.5 cm | ends deep inside the glove's cuff |
| bare arm (`skin`) | −2.2 cm | fills the sleeve mouth, stays 3 mm behind the cuff so no pale ring shows |
| shirt cuff (`jacket2`) | +1.0 cm | stays hidden under the blazer instead of ringing the glove |

Weights, UVs and topology are untouched, so the pose, the seam fix below and the
texture all survive. `extend_blazer_sleeves` and `tuck_shirt_cuffs` are no longer
called (kept in the module, docstrings marked superseded) — running them before
this pass fights it.

## Root cause 2 — the sleeve seam tears (fixed)

BodySlide exports each garment as a *sheet*: at every UV-island edge the row of
vertices is duplicated exactly, and the two rows share no edge. In Skyrim both
rows are skinned to the same bone, so nothing moves. The retarget gives each copy
its own influence list; where the copies disagree, the seam opens as soon as the
arm moves — that is the beige oval on the upper arm and the patches at the
shoulder blades in the screenshots.

`build_models.weld_open_seams` finds those coincident pairs *inside one material*
(0.2 mm tolerance, so a real opening such as the placket, the collar or the
sleeve mouth can never be paired) and averages their influence lists, normals and
positions. Measured on the shipped model: **3252 pairs** (skin 1275, blazer 826,
shirt 740, heels 362, skirt 32, necklace 17).

> This is *not* the seam weld that was rejected earlier (note in HANDOFF §5 /
> MODLOG: "welding the jacket's overlap edges shut distorts the sleeve"). That
> attempt merged the blazer's *overlap* edges, i.e. two different surfaces that
> happen to be close, which folds the sleeve. This pass only ever touches two
> rows that are the same point in space, and it moves no vertex by more than
> 0.1 mm — geometry cannot deform. The posed render shows no spike.

## Root cause 3 — the shirt rides out over the shoulder (mitigated)

Two separate things:

1. **Weights.** The shirt is 100 % arm-weighted across the shoulder while the
   blazer keeps a mixed `Spine2 / Shoulder / Arm` band. `build_models.follow_layer_weights`
   copies the nearest blazer vertex's influence list onto the shirt across the
   shoulder band (z 136–158 cm, 5900 vertices), so the two layers now deform
   together there and the shirt cannot emerge from under the jacket at any pose.
2. **Geometry.** The shirt's collar is as tall as the blazer's and its wing tips
   reach past the blazer's neckline. `build_models.tuck_shirt_collar` nips the
   shirt collar 7 % inward in x and 12 % down above z 150 cm (3997 vertices).
   The factors are deliberately mild: the white V down the chest is the outfit's
   design and stays as wide as it was.

Both maps are continuous at their threshold, so neither can tear the mesh.

## What still needs an in-game look

- **Glove size.** The sleeve now ends 2.5 cm past the hand joint, i.e. inside the
  glove's cuff *if* the glove covers the wrist. If the heister's glove turns out
  to be shorter than that, the fix is one number (`targets["jacket"]`) and a
  rebuild.
- **Shirt cuff.** The white shirt cuff now ends 1 cm short of the hand joint,
  inside the blazer. If a white ring still shows at the glove, lower
  `targets["jacket2"]` (e.g. −1.0).
- **Collar.** The tuck is a nudge, not a redesign. If the wings still flare in
  game, the next step is to raise the *blazer's* collar rather than shrink the
  shirt further (the shirt's V is the outfit's design).

## Known issues NOT addressed in this round

  1.32 is a deliberate user-approved choice).
- Skirt hem is very short; it is the Skyrim source's own cut.

  has no in-game report yet.
- The LAS glove-adapter race in `hooks/businesswoman_las.lua` (audit finding) is
  untouched: with the sleeve now reaching the glove it no longer matters for the
  look, but it still decides whether the vanilla forearm is spawned.
