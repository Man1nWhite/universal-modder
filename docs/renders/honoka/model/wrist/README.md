# Offline wrist render — `OfficeSuite` (D75)

The wrist junction the 2026-11-26 captures showed, rendered offline: the outfit's skin in grey and a
PD2 glove's `g_hands` shell in blue, both skinned and posed with the rotations a weapon pose puts on
a wrist (a 45° forearm twist that carries the hand, plus a 30° bend). Blue over grey is the glove
covering the skin; grey over blue at the crease is skin through the cuff.

| File | Pose | Glove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`officesuite_wrist_ext30_twist.png`](officesuite_wrist_ext30_twist.png) | 30° wrist bend + 45° forearm twist | `npc_criminal_sydney` |

Regenerated by the unified renderer's wrist target, from the build's own model:

```sh
OFFICESUITE_SKIN_MODEL=build/honoko2/officesuite/officesuite.model \
    .venv/bin/python tools/render_mod_previews.py --unified --only-wrist
```

`tools/pd2-las/wrist_compare.py out.png --before MODEL --glove sydney --pose ext30_twist` adds a
"before" panel (any `g_body` model; `git show <rev>:dist/Honoka/OfficeSuite.zip` unpacks to one) and
prints the number below for both.

## What the number says

For each palm-axial station the tool reports the worst of twelve angular sectors by how far the
skin's wall stands outside the glove's, in mm — negative means the glove is still outside the skin,
the good direction. On this pose (Sydney's left wrist):

| station (cm from the crease) | −1.2 | −0.9 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D70 build | −1.5 | −4.5 |
| D75 build | −1.5 | −4.5 |

The pose's own worst sector is elsewhere (the twist moves it round the ring), so the summary figure
comes from the full sweep in `tests/test_wrist_twist.py`: over six wrist poses on both wrists against
three of the game's gloves, the worst sector of skin outside a cuff went from **+2.67 mm** (Sydney)
and **+3.19 mm** (Joy) on the D70 build to **+1.63 mm** and **−1.79 mm** on D75, Clover from −2.61 to
−5.51 mm. The test pins the 0.20 cm bound.

This is a static offline render, not a capture: it reproduces the rotations, not the game's
animation blending or the glove material's own thickness. The in-game check is still the user's.
